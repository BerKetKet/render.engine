## Detailed Root-Cause Analyses

### C1 — Cover Page (`p1`)
- **Observation**: Across `e3_std` (`p1`), `e3_small` (`p1`), `e3_compact` (`p1`), and `e3_large` (`p1`), the cover page has `diff_pixels = 8,739` (`7,178` on `probe` p1; `29,780` on `exam1` p1; `29,609` on `exam2` p1).
- **Measurement**: Both our rendered cover and the device capture span the exact same full-bleed viewport window `x=[0..1079], y=[83..2239]`. On `e3_std` p1, out of `2,329,560` pixels in the comparison window, `2,320,821` pixels (`99.62%`) are **100% identical (`diff == 0`)**, `6,350` pixels have `|diff| == 1`, `2,389` pixels have `|diff| == 2`, and **`0` pixels have `|diff| > 2`**.
- **Root Cause**: The EPUB source stores `cover.png` as a lossless PNG, whereas the KFX book loaded onto the Kindle device transcodes cover images through JPEG compression (`8x8` DCT quantization), introducing `+/-1..2` gray-level mosquito noise within `8 px` of sharp strokes (`x=[75..1007], y=[441..1907]`).

### C2 — Single-Line Page with `97,625 px` Diff in `e09_pagination` (`Std` p62, `Small` p39, `Compact` p41, `Large` p90)
- **Observation**: Every theme of `kindle_exam_v3.epub` has a single page in `e09_pagination` with `render_lines = 1`, `capture_lines = 1`, and `diff_pixels = 97,625`.
- **Measurement**: That page contains the `600 x 2600` tall monochrome test image (`Non-cover image block: (600, 2600)`), which `add_image` scales to fit the page height `maxh = 2036`, producing a `470 x 2036` image placed at `x=[305..774], y=[167..2202]`. Both our renderer and the device capture have the **exact same ink bounding box** `x=[305..774], y=[167..2202]` (`0` position error, `0` size error).
- **Root Cause**: Inside the `470 x 2036` (`956,920 px`) image box, `859,295` pixels (`89.8%`) are identical (`diff == 0`), while `97,625` pixels along the internal black grid lines and borders differ due to KFX JPEG transcoding (`cols 0..7` exhibit 8x8 DCT ringing `[0, 0, 0, 0, 3, 2, 45, 164]` inside the solid black border where any linear/bilinear kernel is `[0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 12, 226]`).

### C3 — `e07_inline` Glyph-Level Horizontal Shifts (`v1.11.2`)
- **Observation**: On `e3_std` p42–47 (`6` pages) and `e3_small` p27–30 (`4` pages), every paragraph had `vertical shift = 0` (`block_residuals.py` reported `+0` on all blocks), yet lines containing `<u>underlined words</u>, <s>struck words</s>, <span class="red">red words</span>, <span class="blue">blue words</span>, <a href="#t">a link</a>, <code>code_0()</code>.` had `2,000–5,200` diff pixels per line.
- **Root Cause**: When an inline decoration tag (`<u>`, `<s>`, `<span class="red">`, `<span class="blue">`) closes immediately before a comma (`,`), `epub.py` emits two separate `Run` objects (`Run("underlined words", underline=True)` and `Run(", ", underline=False)`), which `build_items` places into two directly adjacent `Box` items (`Box("words")` and `Box(",")` with no `Glue` or `Penalty` between them). Because `layout.py:564` previously only applied cross-Box OpenType GPOS kerning when `n1` or `n2` was in `DASH_SPLIT_GLYPHS`, it dropped the `-20 FUnits` (`-44` units in 26.6 fixed point at `45px`) GPOS kern between `'s'` and `'comma'` three times on the same line (`-132` units = `2.06 px`), altering the line's justification slack `delta` and shifting every word on the line by `1 px`.
- **Fix (`v1.11.2`, `commit 1d85e69`)**: Applied cross-Box GPOS kerning across all unspaced inline `Box` joins (`isinstance(items[idx + 1], Box) and not n1[0].isupper()`) sharing the same `Face`.
- **Result**:
  - `e3_std`: `762,672 -> 713,359` diff px (**`-49,313 px`** across `p42..p47`), `lost = 0`.
  - `e3_small`: `879,278 -> 849,015` diff px (**`-30,263 px`** across `p27..p30`), `lost = 0`.

### C4 — Single-Glyph Sub-Pixel Residuals (`v1.11.3`)
- **Observation**: Pages with `135–303 px` total diff have a `1 px` horizontal shift on a **single glyph** in a single justified line, occurring predominantly on first lines (`li == 0`) of `<p class="ti12">` (`text-indent: 1.2em`) paragraphs at `34 px` (`e3_small`) and `39 px` (`Compact`).
- **Root Cause**: At `34 px` (`size26 = 2176`) and `39 px` (`size26 = 2496`), `1.2 * size26` is `2611.2` and `2995.2` (having a `+0.2` fractional 26.6 unit). Truncating `indent26 = int(indent_em * size_px * 64)` discarded `+0.2` before `layout_line` computed `float32` pen positions. On the second word (`m_glues == 1`) of `li == 0`, glyphs whose integer pen landed at `sub == 31` (`31/64 = 0.484375 px`) were actually at `>= 32.0/64` (`0.500 px`) in `float32` once `indent_frac26 = +0.2` and `sp_fl` were included.
- **Fix (`v1.11.3`, `commit fc0cc3d`)**: Passed `indent_frac26 = (indent_em * size_px * 64.0) - indent26` into `layout_line` for the `m_glues == 1` (`sub == 31`) `float32` tie-break check.
- **Result**:
  - `e3_small`: **`29 -> 32/54` exact pages (`+3` exact pages: `p4`, `p37`, `p40`)**, `1514 -> 1518/1651` pos lines (`+4`), `849,015 -> 848,187` diff px (`-828 px`), `lost = 0` everywhere.

### C5 — `e06_structure`, `e08_verse`, and `e04`/`e05` German/French Hyphenation
- **Diagnosed & Classified Only**:
  1. **`e04_hyph_de` (`e3_std` p32–33) & `e05_hyph_fr` (`e3_std` p35)**: Line-break differences caused by 1–2 German/French compound words where KRF's dictionary/pattern hyphenator chooses a different syllable break than our compiled Liang trie.
  2. **`e06_structure` (`e3_std` p38–41)**: Multi-element structural chapter combining nested `<ul>/<ol>`, `<dl>/<dt>/<dd>`, `<blockquote>`, `<pre>`, and tables; vertical residuals accumulate `+1..+7 px` across block boundaries.
  3. **`e08_verse` (`e3_std` p50–51)**: Poetry stanzas where lines exceeding the measure wrap with hanging indentation on the device (`docs/crops/task_c/c5_e08_verse_e3_std_p49.png`).
