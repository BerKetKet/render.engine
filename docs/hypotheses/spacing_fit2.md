# Spacing Settings Device Fit v2 — Experiments & Results (`v1.11.16`)

All runs evaluated under `KINDLE_RENDER_STRICT_HARFBUZZ=1` (HarfBuzz `6.0.0`) via `tools/lab_spacing/score_spacing.py` against `data/spacing_caps` (pages 2–5 summed; page 1 ignored).

- **v1.11.15 Baseline**: Standard `7,584,793`, Compact `6,534,794`, Large `6,909,148`, **GRAND TOTAL `21,028,735`** (vs `v1.11.14` `26,307,438`).
- **v1.11.16 Final**: Standard `4,871,548` (`-2,713,245`), Compact `5,903,914` (`-630,880`), Large `6,909,148` (`0`), **GRAND TOTAL `17,684,610` (`-3,344,125` px vs `v1.11.15`, `-8,622,828` px vs `v1.11.14`)**. Zero cells worsened by `> 0.5%`.
